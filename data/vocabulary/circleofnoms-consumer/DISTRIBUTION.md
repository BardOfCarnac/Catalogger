# Distribution notes — CircleofNoms Consumer Vocabulary

## Attribution

Original homebrew tables: **u/CircleofNoms**.

Host/reference: **Dataterm**, https://montreal.dataterm.ca/en/random-tables/

Dataterm states that the original CircleofNoms material is hosted there with permission. Vend-R does not interpret that statement as a transferable licence for Vend-R redistribution.

## What this pack contains

This package contains Vend-R-normalized commercial vocabulary, classifications, source links, brand-profile relationships, and attribution.

It does **not** contain the original random tables, roll ranges, table ordering, source PDFs, or copied rules prose.

## Release gates

Before public redistribution:

1. Record affirmative permission covering Vend-R's redistribution of the normalized derivative pack.
2. Change `distribution.status` in `source.json` from `permission-pending` to an approved state and record the permission reference.
3. Keep clear attribution to u/CircleofNoms and identify Dataterm as host rather than creator.
4. Keep the pack freely accessible.
5. Keep an obvious notice that the material is unofficial and not approved or endorsed by R. Talsorian Games.
6. Recheck the current R. Talsorian Games Homebrew Content Policy before release: https://rtalsoriangames.com/homebrew-content-policy/

## Separation from Vend-R originals

Do not merge source-derived `VOC-CN-` concepts into the Vend-R-original namespace. Vend-R-created gap-fill vocabulary lives in the sibling `vendr-consumer-extension` pack.
